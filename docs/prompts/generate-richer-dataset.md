## 3. Schema

### dim_region
`region_id, region_name, province, region_manager_name, population_weight`
Weights: Phnom Penh 0.45, Siem Reap 0.15, Sihanoukville 0.15, Kampot 0.15, Battambang 0.10.

### dim_product
`product_id, product_name, category, billing_type, unit_price_usd, price_effective_from`
Products and prices — these must be genuinely different in magnitude, which is the whole point:

| product | category | billing_type | unit_price_usd |
|---|---|---|---|
| SIM Starter | Voice | one_off | 1.50 |
| Top-up Bundle | Voice | one_off | 0.75 |
| Data Pack 5GB | Data | one_off | 2.50 |
| Postpaid Plan M | Voice+Data | monthly | 12.00 |
| Home Internet 30Mbps | Broadband | monthly | 22.00 |
| Enterprise Leased Line | Broadband | monthly | 180.00 |

**Price revision:** `Home Internet 30Mbps` changes from 22.00 to 19.90 effective 2026-01-01. Record both rows with different `price_effective_from`. The sales fact must use the price valid on the transaction date. (This makes joining to a price history table necessary — do not flatten it.)

### dim_rep
`rep_id, rep_name, region_id, hire_date, seniority, target_multiplier`
- 14 reps, Cambodian names, spread across regions.
- `seniority` ∈ {junior, mid, senior}; `target_multiplier` 0.7 / 1.0 / 1.35 accordingly.
- 3 reps hired mid-period (hire_date between 2025-06 and 2026-01) → they must have no sales before hire and partial-year targets.

### fact_sales  (grain: one transaction line, unique txn_id)
`txn_id, txn_date, rep_id, region_id, product_id, customer_id, units, unit_price_usd, gross_amount_usd, discount_usd, net_revenue_usd, calls_attempted, call_outcome`

- `net_revenue_usd = units * unit_price_usd - discount_usd`, rounded to 2dp, exact.
- `units` ≥ 1; Enterprise lines are 1-3, SIM lines 1-20.
- `discount_usd` is 0 most of the time; >0 during two campaign windows (see §5).
- `call_outcome` ∈ {closed_won, follow_up, no_answer, rejected}; ~55% closed_won.
- `calls_attempted` 1-8, and must be *higher* for no_answer/rejected outcomes.

### fact_targets  (grain: month × rep × product)
`month (YYYY-MM), rep_id, product_id, target_revenue_usd, target_units`
Targets must be built so that overall attainment lands around **96% with sd ≈ 12%**, with genuine spread: at least one rep below 75% and one above 118% for the final reporting month. Derive `target = realised revenue / attainment_draw`, using rep-level skill effects — never draw attainment independently per row or the pack will look flat and fake.

### dim_customer
`customer_id, signup_date, province, region_id, plan_type, segment, acquisition_channel, status, churn_date`
- `plan_type` ∈ {prepaid, postpaid, broadband}, roughly 70/20/10.
- `segment` ∈ {consumer, sme, enterprise}, roughly 85/12/3.
- `acquisition_channel` ∈ {retail_store, dealer, online, outbound_tele, referral}.
- `status` ∈ {active, churned}; `churn_date` null iff active.
- Monthly churn 2-4%, **with drivers**: higher for prepaid, higher in year 1, lower for broadband, lower for referral-acquired.

### fact_customer_month  (grain: month × customer, only while customer is active)
`month, customer_id, mrr_usd, data_usage_gb, voice_minutes, sms_count, payment_method, payment_status, support_tickets_opened, nps_score, is_churned_this_month`
- `payment_method` ∈ {cash, bank_transfer, credit_card, mobile_wallet}.
- `payment_status` ∈ {paid, late, failed, partial}.
- Usage must **decline measurably in the 2 months before churn** (this is the early-warning signal the analyst is supposed to find — plant it honestly, do not hide it).
- `nps_score` 1-10, higher for happy/active, lower pre-churn.
- Customers must have **zero rows after their churn month.**

### dim_calendar
`date, fiscal_month, fiscal_quarter, fiscal_year, day_of_week, is_weekend, is_public_holiday, holiday_name`
Include real Cambodian public holidays for 2025-2026: Khmer New Year (13-16 Apr), Pchum Ben (late Sep/early Oct), Water Festival (Nov), International Labour Day (1 May), King's Birthday, Independence Day (9 Nov), Meak Bochea, Visak Bochea.

## 4. Behavioural realism (this is what makes it teachable)

1. **Seasonality on sales volume and revenue**
   - Khmer New Year: activity collapses 60-70% for ~4 days in mid-April; slight pre-holiday stocking spike.
   - Pchum Ben: 20-30% dip for ~3 days.
   - Water Festival: 15% dip in Phnom Penh specifically.
   - Payday effect: daily volume 40-60% above baseline in the last 5 days of each month.
   - Weekend: consumer volume down ~20%, enterprise near zero.
2. **Regional seasonality** — Siem Reap and Sihanoukville peak Nov-Feb (tourism); Phnom Penh is flat; Battambang is rural and flat with lower AOV.
3. **Product mix drift over 18 months** — SIM Starter share falls ~30% relative; Home Internet grows ~60% relative; Enterprise grows slowly but has huge revenue weight. This must show up as a trend the analyst can detect.
4. **Two promo campaigns** with real, dated windows and discounting:
   - `2025-11-01` to `2025-11-30`: "Water Festival Data Blitz" — 25% of Data Pack lines discounted 20-35%.
   - `2026-04-01` to `2026-04-30`: "Khmer New Year Home Internet" — 40% of Home Internet lines discounted 15%.
   Avoid discounting outside these windows.
5. **Rep performance heterogeneity** — rep skill factor drawn once per rep (sd 0.12), applied to all their months. Add a mild learning curve for the 3 mid-period hires.
6. **Correlated noise** — daily revenue should be autocorrelated (AR(1) φ≈0.4) at the rep level, not i.i.d. Pure i.i.d. produces charts no analyst has ever seen in production; it also makes variance analysis trivially boring.
7. **Late/failed payments cluster** — prepaid + cash + low NPS customers are 3x more likely to be `failed`.

## 7. Acceptance tests you must run and report

Write these as assertions inside `scripts/validate_dataset.py` and paste real output:

1. `fact_sales.net_revenue_usd` equals `units * unit_price_usd - discount_usd` (2dp) for all rows outside the 18 planted reversals.
2. Every `txn_date` falls within its rep's `hire_date` and the data period.
3. Each `fact_sales.unit_price_usd` matches the `dim_product` price valid on `txn_date`.
4. `fact_customer_month` has no rows where month > churn month, and no missing months between signup and churn (other than planted nulls).
5. Total realisable revenue for 2026-06 is between $4M and $9M — if it's outside, your scale is wrong; rescale.
6. Monthly churn rate is between 2% and 4% in at least 80% of months.
7. Daily revenue on 2026-04-14 is at least 50% below the trailing 7-day average.
8. The two campaign windows show clearly higher discount rates than non-campaign months.
9. At least one rep in the final month is below 80% attainment and one above 115%.
10. Pre-churn usage decline is statistically detectable: mean `data_usage_gb` in the 2 months before churn is at least 20% below the customer's own baseline.
